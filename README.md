## 📌 Project Overview

This Microsoft Excel project is an interactive **Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard** designed to analyze business revenue, gross profit, cost of goods sold (COGS), sales channels, product categories, regional performance, and country-wise revenue.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through interactive visualizations, key performance indicators (KPIs), Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and slicers to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📑 Dashboard Components

## 🟦 1. Executive Summary & KPI Analysis

Provides an overall view of business performance through key financial and operational metrics.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

• 💰 Total Revenue — $6,45,223

• 📈 Total Gross Profit — $2,96,384

• 📊 Gross Profit Margin — 45.94%

• 📦 Total Units Sold — 6,698

• 🛒 Total Orders — 2,707

• 💵 Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) — $3,48,839

These KPIs help management monitor overall revenue, profitability, sales volume, and business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw business sales data into an interactive Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key performance indicators, analyze revenue and profitability trends, compare geographical performance, and understand sales channel contributions to support effective business decision-making.
